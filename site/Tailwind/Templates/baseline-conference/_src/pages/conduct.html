Code of conduct.

Baseline is a conference for everyone. This short code sets our shared expectations — for attendees, speakers, sponsors, and volunteers.

Short version

Be excellent to each other. Baseline is dedicated to providing a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of gender, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, age, religion, or level of experience. We do not tolerate harassment in any form.

Our expectations

We expect all participants to:

  • ✓ Use welcoming and inclusive language.
  • ✓ Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
  • ✓ Gracefully accept constructive feedback.
  • ✓ Focus on what is best for the community.
  • ✓ Show empathy toward other participants.

Unacceptable behavior

Examples of behavior that will not be tolerated include:

  • ✕ Intimidation, stalking, or following.
  • ✕ Harassing photography or recording.
  • ✕ Sustained disruption of talks or events.
  • ✕ Offensive or sexual language and imagery.
  • ✕ Unwelcome sexual attention or advances.

Scope

This code applies to all Baseline spaces — the venue, the workshop day, the closing party, and any related online spaces (chat, social media, video calls). It applies to attendees, speakers, sponsors, volunteers, and organizers. Sponsors are equally subject to the code and may not use sexualized images or material at their booths.

Reporting

If you are harassed, or notice someone else being harassed, please report it. You can reach the response team any time during the event:

Response team — available 24/7 during the event

conduct@baseline.example · phone in your badge wallet

Reports are confidential. The team will prioritize your safety and can help arrange escorts, contact venue security, or involve local authorities.

Consequences

Participants asked to stop any harassing behavior are expected to comply immediately. Organizers may take any action they deem appropriate, including warning the offender or removing them from the conference without a refund. Repeat or severe violations may result in exclusion from future editions.

Attribution

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant and the Geek Feminism community code of conduct, both used widely across conferences and open-source communities. It is reviewed before each edition.

Questions before the event?

Email conduct@baseline.example — we answer within two working days.
