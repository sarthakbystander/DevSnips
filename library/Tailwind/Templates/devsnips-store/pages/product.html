Stoneware Vessel

By Studio Aalto · Caldas da Rainha, Portugal

A tall, hand-thrown vessel turned on the wheel and fired to 1280° in a wood kiln. Each one leans slightly, carries the marks of its throwing rings, and is finished with a single, deliberate line of glaze down the centre. No two are identical.

Wipe with a soft, dry cloth. The vessel is porous and intended for dry stems — avoid water, which will mark the unglazed body over time. The ash glaze line will deepen with handling; this is intended.

Thrown by Inês Aalto at her studio in Caldas da Rainha, the historic ceramics town of central Portugal. Each vessel is centred, pulled and turned in a single sitting, then dried for ten days before the wood firing.
