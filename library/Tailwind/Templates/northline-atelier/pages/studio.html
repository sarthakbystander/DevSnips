About Northline

A small studio with a wide view of the world.

We are architects, designers and researchers interested in the space between a building and its context. Our work is grounded in observation, careful making and long-term usefulness.

How we work

01 / Observe

We start with climate, movement, material and the ordinary routines that make a place specific.

02 / Edit

We reduce the idea until structure, atmosphere and function reinforce each other.

03 / Make

We stay close to materials and makers, carrying decisions from first sketch to final detail.

Capabilities

From first thought to finished room.

Architecture

New buildings, additions, adaptive reuse and planning.

Interiors

Material palettes, built-ins, lighting and furniture.

Landscape

Gardens, thresholds and relationships to the wider site.

Research

Feasibility, spatial strategy and early-stage design direction.

The team
