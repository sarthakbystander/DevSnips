01

Discover

Continuous, non-intrusive discovery across cloud, DNS, certificates, and source repos — no agents, no credentials to manage. Finds shadow IT the moment it appears.

/ 03 — Lifecycle

A closed loop, not a backlog.

Every exposure moves through five signed states — discover to verify — so nothing lands in a queue and disappears.

  1. 01 ~4m

    Discover

    Non-intrusive scan finds the asset and adds it to inventory.

    state: discovered

  2. 02 ~1m

    Classify

    Tag by cloud, repo, owner, and criticality automatically.

    state: classified

  3. 03 scored

    Prioritize

    EPSS + reachability scoring ranks the exposure by real risk.

    state: prioritized

  4. 04 SLA-bound

    Remediate

    Routed to the owner with a one-click fix and a signed SLA.

    state: remediating

  5. 05 auto

    Verify

    Re-scan confirms the exposure is gone; loop closes automatically.

    state: verified ✓

Every state transition is signed and immutable — exportable to your audit trail.

/ 07 — FAQ

Questions security leaders ask.

Can't find what you need? Talk to an engineer.

Vesper uses passive, non-intrusive techniques — DNS enumeration, certificate transparency logs, and public cloud metadata — to build a complete asset inventory. No agents, no credentials to manage, and no production traffic generated. Read-only connectors are optional for deeper cloud enrichment.

Scanners list findings; Vesper ranks them by real exploitability. Each exposure is enriched with EPSS scores, CISA KEV presence, asset criticality, and internet reachability — so your team fixes the ~6% of findings that actually pose risk, instead of drowning in the 94% that don't.

After an owner marks an exposure fixed, Vesper automatically re-scans the asset to confirm the exposure is truly gone — not just acknowledged. Each verified closure is signed with a SHA-256 hash and added to an immutable audit trail you can export for compliance.

AWS, GCP, and Azure for cloud asset enrichment; GitHub and GitLab for source-repo exposure; Jira, Slack, and GitHub Issues for remediation routing; and SIEM export for your existing SOC workflows. New integrations ship every quarter.
