    Discover the school where true street art is born,and every student becomes a creator who changes how the city looks.

    SPRAY was founded by working artists, not administrators. We don't just teach technique — we help you find a visual voice that holds up outside the classroom, on a real wall, in public.

    Roadmap

    How it actually runs, start to finish.

    1. 1

      Enroll

      P a course, meet your teacher, get your materials list.

    2. 2

      Sketch

      Every piece starts on paper. We won't let you skip this.

    3. 3

      Wall time

      Practice walls first, then supervised legal spots.

    4. 4

      Critique

      Weekly group review — direct, specific, no ego.

    5. 5

      Show

      End-of-term group show, open to the public.
