Privacy, in plain language

We answer one question at a time so you can read top to bottom. Expand another and the first closes automatically.

We collect only what's required to run your account: email, workspace metadata, and billing country. We never track behavior across other sites, and analytics are anonymized and opt-out-able from your settings.

Data is encrypted at rest in the region you select during signup, with replicas confined to that region for residency compliance. You can migrate regions on request without downtime.

Open Privacy, then Request deletion. We confirm by email and permanently remove personal data within 30 days, including backups. A receipt is generated for your records.
