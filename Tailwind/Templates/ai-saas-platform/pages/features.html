Guardrails

Safety that runs before responses

Configure policies once and enforce them across every model and route. Block, redact, or flag content before it ever reaches your users.

  • PII detection & redaction in real time
  • Toxicity & topic restriction policies
  • Block, redact, or flag per route

guardrails.config.ts

const policies = nexus.guardrails.define({
  pii: { mode: "redact", fields: ["ssn", "email"] },
  toxicity: { threshold: 0.8, action: "block" },
  topics: { deny: ["finance"], action: "flag" }
})
