Accessibility & Responsiveness

  • Semantic HTML: header, nav, main, section, article, aside, figure, footer
  • ARIA labels, aria-expanded on toggles, role="tablist" on filter groups
  • Focus-visible rings on all interactive elements
  • prefers-reduced-motion respected for dashboard chart animations
  • Mobile, tablet, and desktop layouts throughout every page

Inclusive by Design

Every interaction is keyboard-accessible

✓ Semantic landmarks for screen readers

✓ High contrast text on light background (WCAG AA)

✓ Touch targets ≥ 44px on mobile

✓ Reduced-motion fallbacks
