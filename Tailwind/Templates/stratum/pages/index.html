/ 03 — Workflow

From invoice in-box to settled payment.

Five steps, one audit trail. No swivel-chair, no spreadsheet reconciliation, no last-minute surprises at month-end.

  1. 1 ~0s

    Capture

    Email or upload invoices. OCR extracts line items and GL coding.

  2. 2 ~4s

    Match

    3-way match against PO and receipt. Exceptions flagged for review.

  3. 3 ~38ms

    Approve

    Policy engine routes to the right signers. Mobile or Slack approval.

  4. 4 same-day

    Pay

    Funds route via ACH, wire, or virtual card by vendor preference.

  5. 5 auto

    Reconcile

    Journal entry posts to your ERP. Bank statement matches instantly.
