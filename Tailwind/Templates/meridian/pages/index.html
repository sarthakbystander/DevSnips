/ 01 — Platform

Three systems, one audit trail.

Pager noise is a symptom of disconnected tools. Meridian unifies alert routing, incident command, and postmortems — so a signal becomes an action without a human triage queue.

PILLAR 01

Route

Ingest alerts from Datadog, Grafana, PagerDuty, or webhooks. Meridian correlates related signals and suppresses duplicates before anyone is paged.

  • Correlation by service + topology
  • Dedupe, group, and silence rules
  • 80+ native integrations
PILLAR 02

Respond

A shared war room for every incident — severity, timeline, responders, and runbook on one screen. Coordinate in Slack or in-app without losing the audit trail.

  • Live timeline + severity model
  • Role assignment + paging
  • Status pages + stakeholder updates
PILLAR 03

Learn

Every resolved incident becomes a structured postmortem with contributing factors, action items, and follow-ups tracked to completion — not a Google Doc nobody reads.

  • Timeline-first postmortem drafts
  • Action items with owners + due dates
  • Blameless review analytics

    Signal

    Alerts land from any source. Meridian tags severity from your ruleset.

  2. 2 ~2s

    Triage

/ 07 — FAQ

Questions on-call teams ask.

Still stuck? Talk to an engineer — not a sales bot.

No. Meridian sits on top of Datadog, Grafana, New Relic, Sentry, and PagerDuty — it ingests their alerts, correlates them, and routes the result. Your monitoring stays where it is; the noise stops reaching the wrong person.

Correlation combines your service topology, deployment markers, and signal fingerprints — alert name, tags, and timing windows. You can tune correlation rules or let the default model run, which learns from acknowledged incidents over the first few weeks.

Yes. Schedules are region-aware and shift with the rotation clock automatically. Stepped escalation (primary → secondary → manager) and conditional rules (sev, service, time-of-day) ensure the right region is paged first, with fallbacks only if no ack within your configured window.

Every state transition is signed and immutable. You can export the full incident timeline and postmortem as JSON or PDF, or stream events to S3, Snowflake, or a SIEM. Meridian is SOC 2 Type II and HIPAA-eligible, with retention configurable per plan.

Most teams route their first real incident within an hour of connecting sources. Noise reduction compounds over the first two weeks as the correlation model tunes to your topology, and the majority of teams report a measurable drop in pages inside the first quarter.
