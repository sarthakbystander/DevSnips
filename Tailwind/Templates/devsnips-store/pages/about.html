The atelier

A small store
for objects that stay.

DevSnips began in 2019 as a single shelf of pots in a Lisbon bookshop. Today it is a tiny operation — four people, fourteen studios, six countries — held together by one belief: that the objects we live with should be made by hand, made to last, and made to age well.

We do not chase trends or seasons. We release in volumes, slowly, when something is ready.

How we work

Three principles, no slogans.

01

Make it to be kept.

We design for the long second look, not the first. If an object cannot outlast a trend, we do not make it.

02

Name the maker.

Every piece carries the studio that made it, where it was made, and the material it came from. Provenance is not a footnote.

03

Stay narrow.

Five materials, four categories. We would rather know a few things deeply than many things poorly.

From studio to shelf

The slow path of an object.

  1. 01 · Studio

    Commission

    We brief a studio we trust, or they bring us a piece. Nothing is designed by committee.

  2. 02 · Making

    Small batch

    Made in runs of twelve to forty. We visit, we watch, and we wait for the kiln to cool.

  3. 03 · Provenance

    Catalogued

    Each piece is numbered, photographed, and given a maker card. We keep the records.

  4. 04 · Yours

    Sent

    Packaged by hand in recycled wool and card, shipped carbon-neutral, and cared for after.
