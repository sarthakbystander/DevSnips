FAQ

Common questions

Short answers about early access, pricing, and how Ledger handles your data.

When does early access start?

We plan to open the first batch in Q3 2026. Waitlist members are invited in small groups so we can support onboarding properly.

Is there a free trial?

Early access includes a 30-day trial on the Team plan. After that, founding pricing applies if you continue.

Can we self-host?

Not during early access. Self-hosted options may come later for Business customers. We’ll share details when that path is ready.

Where is data stored?

Primary storage is in the EU (Frankfurt). We do not sell customer data. Full details will be in the privacy policy published before general availability.

Which tools can we connect?

At launch: Intercom, Zendesk, Linear, and email forwarding. More integrations are planned based on early-access feedback.

How is priority scoring calculated?

You set weights for impact, effort, and segment. Scores update as you change weights or add evidence — no black-box ranking.

Can stakeholders view without a full seat?

Yes on Team and Business. Share a filtered, read-only link. They don’t consume a paid seat during early access.

Still have questions?

Join the waitlist and mention your use case. We’ll reply when we open your batch.
