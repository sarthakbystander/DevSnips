Features

Everything you need to decide with confidence

Ledger is built around three jobs: capture signals, score them fairly, and keep the roadmap honest.

Unified intake

Import from Intercom, Zendesk, Linear, and email. Tag by segment, plan, and source so nothing stays orphaned in a spreadsheet.

Priority scoring

Combine impact, effort, and revenue signals into a transparent score. Adjust weights per team without rewriting formulas.

Theme grouping

Cluster related requests into themes. Spot patterns early instead of treating every ticket as an isolated ask.

Roadmap links

Connect decisions to releases and public roadmap items. Stakeholders see the same rationale you used in the room.

Decision log

Every prioritization change is recorded with context. Useful for audits, handoffs, and “why did we ship that?” moments.

Lightweight sharing

Share a filtered view with leadership or customers. No extra seats required for read-only access during early access.
