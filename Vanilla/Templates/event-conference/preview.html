01 — About

A conference for people who build the interface

Field Notes Conf is a two-day, single-track conference for the engineers and designers who care about how software actually feels to use. No marketing track, no sponsored keynotes—just practitioners sharing what they learned shipping real products.

Talks cover design systems, front-end architecture, accessibility, documentation, and the operational software most of us actually build. The format is dense: 30-minute talks, 10-minute breaks, long lunches for conversation.

The third edition moves to Lisbon, with a workshop day on March 17 for hands-on design-system and accessibility sessions.

04 — Venue

Where you’ll be

The conference is held at the Altitude Hall, a converted printing house in the Marvila district—a 10-minute metro ride from Lisbon city centre. Single-track stage, dedicated Q&A mic, and a catered common area for the long lunches.

An accessibility entrance is on the east side, with step-free access to the stage and seating. We’ll share a detailed accessibility guide two weeks before the event.
